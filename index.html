PC Building Knowledge Base

Gaming vs Workstation

Gaming PCs prioritize fast single-core CPU speeds and powerful GPUs. Workstations prioritize massive multi-core CPUs and huge amounts of RAM for rendering.

Threadripper & Xeon Warning ⚠️

  • AMD Threadripper: Ultra high-end workstation CPU. Built for 3D rendering and film simulation. NOT recommended for gaming.
  • Intel Xeon: Server CPU meant for data centers and 24/7 hosting. NOT recommended for gaming.

The Core Components

  • CPU (Processor): The brain. AMD's AM5 socket is currently best for future upgrades. Intel's LGA1700 is great but at the end of its lifecycle.
  • GPU (Graphics): The muscle. Handles everything you see on screen. NVIDIA is best for streaming/rendering. AMD often offers better raw gaming value.
  • RAM (Memory): Short-term workspace. 16GB is the minimum. 32GB is the new sweet spot for gaming and multitasking.
  • SSD (Storage): Where games live. NVMe SSDs are mandatory for modern PCs.
