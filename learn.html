The CPU (The Brain)

The CPU processes every click and calculation. More "Cores" mean the PC can do more things at once. For gaming, you want speed; for video editing, you want cores.

RAM (Short-term Memory)

Think of RAM like your desk space. If you have a small desk, you can only work on one paper at a time. 16GB is a standard desk; 32GB is a massive workstation desk where you can have games, Chrome, and Discord open all at once.

The GPU (The Muscle)

This handles the graphics. If you want high FPS or 4K resolution, this is the most important part of your build.
